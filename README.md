# My Favorite Movie: Harry Potter 🪄

## About the Movie

**Harry Potter** is my favorite movie series.
It is about a young wizard named Harry Potter
who studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft
and Wizardry.

*I love this series because it has magic,
friendship, adventure, and exciting stories.*

## My Favorite Characters

1. **Harry Potter** - He is brave and loyal to his friends.
2. **Hermione Granger** - She is smart and hardworking.
3. **Ron Weasley** - He is funny and a good friend.
4. **Albus Dumbledore** - He is wise and powerful.

## Things I Like About Harry Potter

- The magical world of Hogwarts
- The friendship between Harry, Ron, and Hermione
- The exciting adventures
- The different spells and magical creatures
